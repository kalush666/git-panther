# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-11-03 · Status: Accepted

## Context
Git Panther will make many decisions that are hard to reverse. Future contributors (human and AI) need to know why things are the way they are.

## Decision
Record significant decisions as short ADRs in `docs/adr/NNNN-title.md` (Context · Decision · Consequences). ADRs are immutable once accepted; a change is a new ADR that supersedes the old one.

## Consequences
Small writing overhead per decision; much cheaper onboarding and fewer re-litigated debates.
